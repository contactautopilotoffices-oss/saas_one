# Autopilot Procurement Module — Demo Video Script

> **Target Duration:** 4–5 minutes  
> **Format:** Screen recording + voiceover  
> **Audience:** Facility managers, property admins, procurement teams

---

## 1. HOOK — The Problem (0:00–0:25)

**[VISUAL]:** Fast montage of messy WhatsApp chats, scattered Excel sheets, and overdue maintenance tickets.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Managing supplies for properties is chaotic. Requests get lost in chats, approvals take forever, and nobody knows if the budget is already blown. What if your entire procurement flow — from request to delivery — lived right inside your work order system?"

**[VISUAL]:** Clean transition to the Autopilot dashboard. Cursor clicks into a ticket.

---

## 2. SUPER ADMIN SETUP — "Supply Controls" (0:25–1:20)

**[VISUAL]:** Logged in as Super Admin. Navigate to **Procurement Management** page. The "Settings" tab is active.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Before anyone raises a request, the Super Admin configures the ground rules per property. Let's open Supply Controls."

### 2A — Approval Chain
**[VISUAL]:** Scroll to the **Order Approvals** card.
- Set **Approval Limit Amount**, e.g., ₹5,000.
- Assign **Manager A** for requests *below* the limit.
- Assign **Manager B** for requests *at or above* the limit.
- Click **Save Approval Settings**.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Set a threshold. Requests under ₹5,000 go to Manager A for a quick sign-off. Anything above routes to Manager B. No manual decision-making — the system routes automatically."

### 2B — Budget Accounts
**[VISUAL]:** Scroll to **Money Accounts** card.
- Show two accounts: **Repair & Maintenance** and **General**.
- Set monthly limits, e.g., ₹50,000 for RnM.
- Show the colored progress bar and "Spent" counter.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Next, allocate monthly budgets. You get two dedicated accounts — Repair & Maintenance and General. The moment an order is marked received, the spent amount updates automatically. You always know what's left."

### 2C — Price Visibility (Optional)
**[VISUAL]:** Scroll to **Price Visibility** card.
- Show role toggles: Technicians, Staff, Security, Client, etc.
- Mention that Super Admin, Procurement, and Master Admin always see prices.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "You also control who sees what. Hide prices from tenants or junior staff, while keeping full visibility for admins and procurement teams."

---

## 3. RAISING A MATERIAL REQUEST — From Ticket to Cart (1:20–2:30)

**[VISUAL]:** Switch to a staff user's view. Open a maintenance ticket (e.g., "Lobby light fixture repair").

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Now let's see it from the field. A technician is assigned a ticket and realizes he needs parts. Instead of calling or texting, he clicks one button."

**[VISUAL]:** Cursor clicks the **"Material Request"** button (green, next to Flow Map). The **"Buy Items"** modal opens.

### 3A — Browse Catalog
**[VISUAL]:** Show the catalog grid.
- Search bar at the top: type "LED bulb."
- Category chips: Electrical, Plumbing, Cleaning, etc.
- Items show image, name, unit, and estimated price.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The store opens. He can search the existing catalog, filter by category, and add items directly to the cart."

**[VISUAL]:** Click the **+** button on a "20W LED Bulb." Cart badge updates.

### 3B — Custom Item (If needed)
**[VISUAL]:** Click **"Add Custom Item."**
- Fill: Name, Quantity, Unit, Description.
- Attach a photo from site.
- Optionally paste a purchase link.
- Add to cart.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Can't find what he needs? He adds a custom item on the spot, snaps a photo for clarity, and even drops a vendor link."

### 3C — Checkout / Finalize
**[VISUAL]:** Click the sticky bottom bar / **Checkout** button.
- Review screen shows all items with quantities and totals.
- Select **Budget Type**: toggle between **RnM** and **General**.
- Select **Assignee**: a procurement user from the dropdown.
- Click **Place Order**.

**[VISUAL]:** Success overlay appears:
> ✅ **Order Placed!**  
> "Your request is sent to the manager for approval."

**VOICEOVER:**
> "He reviews the cart, picks whether it hits the Repair & Maintenance account or General fund, assigns it to a procurement user, and sends it. Done."

---

## 4. THE APPROVAL FLOW — Manager's View (2:30–3:15)

**[VISUAL]:** Switch to the approver's (Manager A) account. Open **Procurement Management**.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Meanwhile, the assigned manager gets notified. He opens Procurement Management and sees the My Approvals tab."

**[VISUAL]:** The **"My Approvals"** tab shows a badge count. Click it.
- List shows the pending request: ticket number, title, total amount.
- Status badge: **Waiting**.
- Click to expand timeline: PENDING → APPROVED → ORDERED → DELIVERED.

**[VISUAL]:** Click the request card. Detail panel opens on the right.
- Shows: Ordered By, Account (RnM), items list with photos.
- Two action buttons: **Reject** and **Approve**.

**[VISUAL]:** Click **Approve**.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "He sees every detail — who requested it, what's inside, and which budget it hits. One click to approve. Requests above the threshold would have automatically landed on Manager B instead."

---

## 5. PROCUREMENT USER — Order to Delivery (3:15–4:00)

**[VISUAL]:** Switch to Procurement user's view. Open **Procurement Dashboard**.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Once approved, the procurement team takes over. Their dashboard gives full visibility."

**[VISUAL]:** Show dashboard tabs:
- **Overview**: stats and activity feed.
- **Requests**: filter by status, property, date range.
- Click into the newly approved request.

**[VISUAL]:** Show request detail. Status is now **Approved**.
- Procurement user updates status to **Ordered** (via dropdown or action).
- Later, when items arrive, updates to **Delivered**.

**[VISUAL]:** Switch back to the original ticket page.
- Scroll to **Materials** section.
- Show the material request timeline: Pending → Approved → Ordered → Received.
- Total amount visible to admin/procurement roles.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "They mark it ordered, and later, received. The ticket page updates in real time, so the technician knows his parts are on the way — or already at site. The budget also deducts automatically on delivery."

---

## 6. CATALOG MANAGEMENT — The Back Office (4:00–4:30)

**[VISUAL]:** Back as Procurement user or Super Admin. Open the catalog modal in management mode.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Admins and procurement users also maintain the store."

**[VISUAL]:** Show:
- **Add Item**: upload photo, set name, price, unit, category.
- **Edit** existing item inline.
- **Bulk Upload**: drag an Excel/CSV file. AI auto-maps columns and imports 500 items in seconds.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Add new items with photos and pricing. Edit on the fly. Or bulk-upload an entire vendor catalog — the AI maps your columns automatically."

---

## 7. OUTRO — Summary & CTA (4:30–5:00)

**[VISUAL]:** Split screen showing all four views:
- Left: Staff raising request from ticket.
- Center-top: Manager approving.
- Center-bottom: Procurement user tracking.
- Right: Super Admin budgets & settings.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "From the field to the finance desk, Autopilot's Procurement module connects every step. No lost requests. No budget surprises. Just a clear, accountable flow from material request to delivered item."

**[VISUAL]:** Fade to Autopilot logo + tagline.

> **"Autopilot — Run Your Property on Autopilot."**

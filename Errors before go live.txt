The Root Cause
While we limited the ticket feed to 150, I previously instructed the cache to save ALL visited properties (and their 150 tickets each) to the device's local disk cache (AsyncStorage). If you, as a super admin, switch between 5 different properties, it tries to save 5 completely full dashboards into local storage all at once. This quickly hits the Android SQLite 6MB limit.

When AsyncStorage throws the SQLITE_FULL error, it fails to save the new state and crashes the storage layer, which causes the dashboard to aggressively roll back and reload the "old" property you were just on.

The Fix
I have just updated dashboardStore.ts to stop saving the background property cache to the disk.
